# PART 2 — 3-EMAIL OUTREACH SEQUENCE

**Target:** HR managers and operations directors at Canadian companies of 50–500 employees, particularly those with a known training need (recent hiring wave, new site, regulatory change, no internal L&D function).

---

## Email 1 — Day 1 (The Opener)
**Purpose:** Relevance check tied to a personalised trigger. Get a yes/no, not a sale.

**Subject options (A/B test):**
- `specialist trainer sourcing — [Company]`
- `finding a [topic] trainer for [Company]`
- `quick question for [First Name]`

---

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Noticed [Company] is [personalised trigger: hiring across [team] / opened a new site in [City] / recently expanded into [region]] — usually a sign training requirements are landing on someone's desk.

We have just launched a Specialist Trainer Sourcing service for Canadian employers in the 50–500 employee range. Short version: you give us a brief — audience, topic, dates, what "qualified" looks like — and we find, vet, and place the trainer. Topics where the instructor's qualifications and practical experience actually matter: statistics, regulatory and compliance, sector-specific operations, leadership, emerging tech.

Our fee is contingent on placement. If we cannot find someone who fits, you do not pay.

Worth a 15-minute call, or not on your radar right now?

[Your name]
Geecom Learning | https://geecomlearning.com/trainer-sourcing.html

---

**Notes:**
- ~128 words. Do not add more.
- The closing line is a yes/no — make it easy to say "not now."
- Personalise the trigger to a real signal (hiring post, new location, leadership change, regulatory update). Do not send if you cannot find one.

Whether or not we end up working together, one observation that might be useful:

We screen for delivery capability before anyone goes on a shortlist — recent sessions, references, and a working call where we see how they handle a room.

If you ever have a brief where the trainer's credibility and delivery matter more than the platform, the page below covers how the service works. No pitch, just a useful reference.

## Sequence Rules

- Three touches total. Do not add a fourth without a fresh trigger.
- Stop the sequence the moment someone replies, books a call, or unsubscribes.
- Personalise the Email 1 trigger every single time. Generic openers kill reply rates.
- Include Geecom Learning's physical mailing address and a one-click unsubscribe in the email footer (CASL compliance).
- Verify every email address through NeverBounce or ZeroBounce before sending.
- No attachments. No calendar links in Email 1.
